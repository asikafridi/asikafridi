the origin

It starts with a question, not a keyboard.

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering student at Daffodil International University (2024 — present). Long before the syntax, I liked pulling things apart to see why they worked — that instinct is still the engine.

the problem-solver

Competitive programming taught me how to think.

On Codeforces and in contest practice I've worked through 200+ problems — each one a small exercise in staying calm, finding structure, and turning a messy prompt into a clean, provable solution.
